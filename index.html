AI scoring model
5 signals. Weights that shift by strategy.
eKhaya doesn't use a fixed algorithm. The score weights shift depending on your investment strategy - because a great flip and a great rental are fundamentally different deals.
Optimized for buy-and-hold investors. Rental yield and neighborhood trajectory are weighted highest - a property that cash-flows well in a stable or improving area is the core target.
Total weight 100 pts
Why these weights
Rental yield drives long-term returns, so it leads. Neighborhood trajectory matters for appreciation and tenant quality. Price discount is still important but secondary — a slightly above-market property in a great location can still cash-flow well.
